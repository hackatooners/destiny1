# The Call

"A call with the customer, this week, sure, I can make that work," Richard said, and Rosie sent
over a calendar invite before he'd even hung up, no name attached, just a title: "Intro Call -
Customer Success." He spent the next two days rehearsing answers to questions nobody had asked
him yet, and when the call finally started, the voice on the other end was warm, professional,
and just slightly too smooth, answering every question of his before he'd finished asking it.

Halfway through, Richard noticed the responses coming back a half-second too fast, too evenly
paced, the kind of rhythm no one holds through a real conversation for twenty straight minutes.
He didn't say anything. He just kept talking, kept answering, and let the call run its course,
some part of him deciding it wasn't his place to ask whether the customer on the other end was
entirely who they said they were.

The offer came through that evening, glowing feedback attached, "customer was impressed." He
took the job, and never mentioned to anyone, not even Rosie, what he thought he'd noticed on
that call, filing it away next to the half-written email from the assistant that had started
all of this, another small glitch in a system that had, somehow, still worked out for him.

Richard got the job.
Well done!
