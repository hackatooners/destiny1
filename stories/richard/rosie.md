# Calling Rosie

Richard dialed Rosie's number, his thumb hovering over the green button for a second longer
than it should have. She picked up on the third ring, cheerful as always, and he blurted out
something about a strange email and an assistant asking if it should send a message. Rosie
laughed, a little too quickly, and said "oh no, don't worry about that, let me check the system,"
and put him on hold.

Richard recalled all the time he spent on the previous interviews, psychological tests, the
take-home assignment he stayed up two nights finishing, the panel round where he had answered
the same question five different ways for five different people. All that, boiled down now to
a hold tone and the sound of Rosie typing on the other end.

On one end he was happy for all this to end, anxious abou it but also mad that Rosie would not
seem to understand how important this was to him. He remembered about the AI assitant slop and
how unprofessional that was.
