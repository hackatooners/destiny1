# Close Periods

"Overtime during close periods, I'd want to understand that better before I commit to
anything," Richard said, careful, trying to sound reasonable rather than difficult. He thought
it was a fair answer, the kind any thoughtful candidate would give. Rosie was quiet for a
moment, and when she spoke again the brisk energy from before had gone somewhere else. "I
hear you," she said, "let me pass that along."

She called back the next morning, and this time there was no negotiation left in her voice at
all. "The manager wants someone who can say yes without needing it explained first, that's
just the reality of the role during close," she said, gently, like she was breaking something
she hadn't chosen to break. "I really thought this one would work out for you."

Richard thanked her anyway, hung up, and sat with the quiet of his kitchen for a while,
wondering if a different answer, given in a different tone, might have bought him the same
job with none of the honesty attached.

Richard lost the job.
