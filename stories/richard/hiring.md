# The Negotiation

Rosie came back on the line with a different energy this time, brisker, like she was reading
off a list someone had handed her. "Okay, the hiring manager is willing to reconsider, German
or not, but he wants to know a few things before he signs off," she said. "Are you okay
traveling on short notice, sometimes with a day or two heads up? Is overtime during close
periods something you can commit to? And would you be comfortable taking a call directly with
the customer this week, before the offer is finalized?"

Richard felt the door that had barely opened suddenly swing wide, three separate hinges he
hadn't expected, each one asking for something different. He knew he couldn't promise
everything and mean it, not without knowing which of these actually mattered to the manager and
which were just Rosie covering herself, reading a script someone in HR had written for exactly
this kind of call.

"Which one do you want me to answer first," he asked, and Rosie said it didn't matter, the
manager just wanted one clear yes to build the offer around.
